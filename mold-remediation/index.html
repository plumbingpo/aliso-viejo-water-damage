Beyond looking bad, mold endangers health and tells you there is a moisture problem underneath, one that can weaken the building itself. Children, older adults and anyone with a weakened immune system are hit hardest by exposure, which can bring headaches, allergic reactions, breathing trouble and worse. Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

First comes a full assessment of the property, frequently with air quality tests that identify the kinds of spores in the air and how concentrated they are. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air
  • Keep gutters clean so rainwater does not seep in along the foundation

Warning Signs for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Moisture beading up again and again on glass, walls or anything cold
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
